POWER BI DATA MODEL

Candidates (fact-like application table)
CandidateID, CandidateName, Gender, Education, ExperienceYears, City, JobRole, Source, Skills, ResumeScore, ApplicationDate, Status, Shortlisted, TimeToHireDays

CandidateSkills (bridge/detail table)
CandidateID, Skill, Status, JobRole

Relationship: Candidates[CandidateID] 1 ---- * CandidateSkills[CandidateID]
